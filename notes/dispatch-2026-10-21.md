**Title:** #506 Phase A — the raft rides the SWE: one water datum for floating and tipping, a raft OOB envelope, forces mounted by the vehicle

**Context / motivation**

Issue #506 (10-18, P2 Content) makes the raft a finished mode and the delta a finale. Phase A is its foundation and is independent of every file today's Fix First swarm and the two open PRs touch except one shared helper (see Open questions). Verified on `main` `971cce8`:

- Buoyancy and drag already read the SWE: `src/systems/water/waterForceBridge.ts` (`stagedWaterLevel`) and the worker hull mirror. But the main-thread `isFloating` / tipping check in `src/vehicles/RaftVehicle/hooks/raftPhysicsRuntime.ts` compares against the constant `WATER_PHYSICS.LEVEL`. When a `hydroEvent` lowers the stage, the hull sinks correctly and the tipping logic thinks it is still afloat; when it raises the stage, a raft resting on a bar is "floating".
- The raft shares the runner's OOB envelope (`src/systems/map/segmentFrames.ts`; `docs/reference/SURVIVAL_LAYER.md` "Future"), so a wide pond section can wipe a raft that is merely drifting laterally.
- The raft's force systems (`WaterFlowForces`, `VortexForceSystem`, `VehicleTuner`) are mounted from `src/components/TrackManager.tsx` (~`:632-637`) rather than from the vehicle. PR #512/#513 found that `WaterFlowForces` and `VehicleTuner` currently do nothing: they filter on `segment.active`, which `SegmentData` does not have, and enabling them enables a centering force that always pushes toward `+lateral`.

**Proposed approach (first pass)**

1. **One water datum.** Expose the staged water level the hull sample already uses (the bridge's `stagedWaterLevel` or a `getStagedWaterLevelAt(x, z)` on the same owner) and make `raftPhysicsRuntime`'s `isFloating`, tipping and `resetRaft` safe-spot logic read it. `WATER_PHYSICS.LEVEL` leaves the runtime path (it may remain as the spawn/fallback constant). Unit test with a stub bridge: a `hydroEvent` that lowers the stage by 0.5 m flips `isFloating` on the same frame the hull sample does.
2. **Raft OOB envelope.** `segmentFrames` gains a vehicle-aware envelope: for the raft, a wider lateral margin and a depth-based (stage-relative) floor instead of a Y-based one. Runner behaviour unchanged (existing tests stay green; add one asserting runner/raft envelopes differ on a `pond` segment).
3. **Forces mount from the vehicle.** `RaftVehicle.tsx` mounts `WaterFlowForces` / `VortexForceSystem` / `VehicleTuner`; the `TrackManager` mount lines go. While moving them, resolve the dead filter: replace `segment.active` with the real liveness predicate (`ChunkManager` / segment pool state) and gate the centering force behind an explicit tuning constant that defaults to off, with the reason in a comment.

**Acceptance criteria (rough)**

- [ ] `raftPhysicsRuntime` test: stage −0.5 m ⇒ `isFloating` false on the same frame as the hull sample; stage +0.5 m over a bar ⇒ true. No reference to `WATER_PHYSICS.LEVEL` remains in `raftPhysicsRuntime.ts` / `useRaftControls.ts` runtime paths (`git grep` shows only spawn/constants).
- [ ] `segmentFrames` test: a raft at lateral offset that wipes a runner on a `pond` segment is in bounds for the raft; a raft 2 m below stage is out.
- [ ] `TrackManager.tsx` no longer imports or mounts the three raft force systems; `RaftVehicle.tsx` does; `?vehicle=raft` on the delta still plays and the vortex (hydro map) still pulls.
- [ ] `WaterFlowForces` / `VehicleTuner` either apply forces under a real liveness predicate or are documented as intentionally inert with the constant that turns them on.
- [ ] `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint` (≤ current warnings), `pnpm test` green; `node scripts/check-build-manifest.mjs --budget` passes (raft is a lazy chunk already); `pnpm test:visual-smoke` unchanged.
